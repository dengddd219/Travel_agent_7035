### GUI Agent Intern (实习生)

### Job Responsibilities
---
* **Participate in requirement analysis, solution design, and version iteration** for GUI Agent-related products; assist in driving the verification, optimization, and implementation of key model and Agent capabilities.
* **Participate in defining data and model performance metrics**; assist in data collection/annotation specifications, sample inspections, and bad case summarization, supporting the advancement of the training-evaluation-iteration loop.
* **Build and maintain the evaluation system**, including the construction of evaluation datasets, organization of metric definitions, execution of evaluation processes, and results analysis, supporting model performance assessment and version acceptance.
* **Possess excellent competitor research and analysis skills**; stay updated on technical and product trends in the AI field, especially regarding multimodal and GUI Agents, providing valuable insights and actionable plans for the team.
* **Demonstrate clear logical expression and strong copywriting skills**; be able to independently complete GTM (Go-To-Market) materials and collaborate with pre-sales, business, and other roles to promote the commercialization of models.

### Job Requirements
---
* **Currently enrolled in a Bachelor's degree program or above** (candidates available for long-term internships are preferred); majors in Computer Science, Software Engineering, Artificial Intelligence, or related fields are preferred; understand the basic methodologies of an AI Product Manager.
* **Have a strong interest in LLM, Multimodal, and Agent directions**; understand the basic concepts and workflows of GUI Agents; familiarity with foundational modules such as tool calling, memory/context, multi-agents, and RAG is highly preferred.
* **Strong learning ability**; willing to read academic papers and experience domestic and overseas products, capable of translating these experiences into clear product documentation, evaluation conclusions, or optimization suggestions.
* **Possess basic data analysis and problem-solving skills**; able to organize evaluation results, summarize bad cases, and deliver structured conclusions.
* **Prompt Engineering skills are a plus**; able to write and iterate prompts or task scripts used for evaluation and verification.
* **Strong communication and collaboration awareness**, excellent execution skills, and the ability to work cross-functionally with multiple teams to drive task completion in a fast-paced environment.
